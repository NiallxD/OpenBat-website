Our Story

During a research project to use passive acoustics to document bat species presence in the Squamish Estuary, our team built an audio processing pipeline around the NABat ML2 model in order to process the large volume of audio being collected. At the same time, our project lead, Niall, was tinkering with self-build, low cost, bat detectors and was curious to see if it was possible to integrate this open-source machine learning model into an iOS app for the purposes of suggesting IDs for bat species.

The OpenBat app emerged from this curiosity.
