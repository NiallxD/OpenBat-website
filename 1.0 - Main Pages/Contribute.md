## Contribute to OpenBat

The community is at the hear of this project, and it doesn't exist without people with an interest in bats getting involved. This is by design, we want people to be involved!

There are two main ways to get involved:

### Bat Field Guide

The OpenBat app contains a global bat field guide. With just over 1,400 bat species known globally, this is not an easy task, and definitely not something our project team can complete.

We are calling on bat experts and nerds from around the world to contribute their knowledge of local bat species to our database, directly building the field guide. Contributing is fairly straight forward and we are working on ways to improve further.

Visit our [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/NiallxD/OpenBat) to learn more.

### Contribute Recordings

OpenBat has the machinery to collect bat recordings from your device, when you allow, so that we can build a reference library of bat calls from around the world. This is not a new initiative, but we are doing it a little different. 

Unlike many other excellent reference call libraries which use high quality research-grade microphones and techniques, the OpenBat Reference Call Library is build using affordable, consumer-grade, hardware. Furthermore, our library collects calls from bats which are going about their day(night) so the nuance of the environmental variables are baked into the calls.

To get started download the app, opt-in to contributing recordings, and head out!
