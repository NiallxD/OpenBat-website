## What is OpenBat?

OpenBat is a free iOS app that turns a compatible ultrasonic USB microphone into a live bat detector! It captures audio at up to 384 kHz, shows a real-time spectrogram, detects individual echolocation pulses and, where an available open-source machine learning model exists for the region, suggests species IDs for the bats you are hearing, all on-device (currently for North America and Canada with UK/Europe in beta).

## Our Story

During a research project to use passive acoustics to document bat species presence in the Squamish Estuary, our team built an audio processing pipeline around the NABat ML2 model in order to process the large volumes of audio being collected. At the same time, the project lead was tinkering with self-build, low cost, bat detectors and was curious to see if it was possible to integrate this open-source machine learning model into an iOS app for the purposes of suggesting IDs for bat species.

The OpenBat app emerged from this curiosity.

## The Challenge

To quote the inspirational Sir David Attenborough:

> *“No one will protect what they don’t care about, and no one will care about what they have never experienced.”*

Bats are difficult to experience. 

There often small, fly at night, rarely perch in line of sight, and they call at frequencies far above those which humans can hear. For all intents and purposes, they are invisible.

 Fear not! There is a way!

Electronics hold the key to this problem. By utilising special microphones, we can design tools which can hear the bat calls, and then repeat those calls in frequencies we can here, in real time. This allows us mere humans to listen to bats as they go about their business in the dead of night.

There is a catch...

Most tools for identifying bat calls are expensive, proprietary, and hard to get hold of, which puts this experience out of reach for a lot of people who'd genuinely enjoy it. Free apps that work with ultrasonic microphones already exist, but as far as we know, none of them use the open-source machine learning models that have been trained on bat echolocation calls. By building that identification directly into the app, OpenBat helps people put a name to the call they just heard, a small moment of recognition that builds a real connection with bats, and a bit more respect for them too.

Bats are remarkable creatures which have adapted ways to experience the world that can only be described as magical! However, they are severely misunderstood. 

Millions of people still fear bats. They believe that they spread diseases, drink human blood, and offer little to our lives. They are seen as pests. This couldn't be further from the truth!
