## Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, corrections, or just to tell us you heard your first bat — all of it is welcome. OpenBat is a small project rather than a company, so replies come from a person and occasionally take a few days.

## Something isn't working

Most of the common ones are answered on the [Help](/help/) page — a red status indicator, a sample rate lower than expected, pulses not triggering, or recordings you can't find.

If none of those fit, email us. It saves a round trip if you include:

- **Your device and iOS version** — Settings > General > About.
- **Which microphone and adapter** you're using.
- **What the status indicator and sample rate say** when the problem happens.
- **What you expected instead**, in a sentence.

A screenshot of the detector screen is worth several paragraphs, if you can get one.

## Adding to the field guide

The field guide is community-contributed, and the fastest route in is the [[Guide Editor|Field Guide Editor]] rather than an email — it opens the guide in ordinary form fields and handles the submission for you. The [Contribute](/contribute/) page walks through what a good entry looks like.

If you'd rather talk it through first, or you have knowledge of a region the guide barely covers, write to us and we'll help you get started.

## Getting something wrong

If the app has misidentified a species, or a field guide entry contradicts what you know locally, we would genuinely like to hear about it — that feedback is how both the models and the guide improve. A recording or a spectrogram attached to the email makes it far easier to act on.

## What we can't help with

We can't identify recordings on request, and we can't advise on licences or permits for bat work in your country. Your national or regional bat group is the right first stop for both — they'll know local law and local species far better than we do.
