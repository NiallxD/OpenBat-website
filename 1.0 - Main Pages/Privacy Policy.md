## What data do we collect?

Our Project collects the following data:

- A UUID (Universally User Identifier) which is generated by your device when you first use the app and is stored on our servers. When you request for your data to be deleted, we store this UUID along with your request for 365 days for audit purposes. A UUID looks like this: 87C4D162-8E61-4D70-9AN6-39ADQ1UFB7FA
- Your accurate (or fuzzy - your choice) location when you are making recordings using the 'Session' mode. This is so that we can assign a location to the bat recording to show it on a map, and to enable you to upload to other community science platforms. This is only stored locally unless you choose to upload a recording to our community science project.
- Your location on app launch to suggest a local AutoID model.
- Audio recordings when you are using the record feature of the app. This is to collect ultrasonic bat calls which stay local only unless you choose to contribute to our community science project. If you do upload a recording, we cut out all sound below 12khz below which human voices emit - we can't hear you, only the bats.
- Your name, or chosen identifier, if you choose to add one. This is added to the metadata of recordings to credit you with the recording. Optional. UUID is used if no name is given.

## How do we collect your data?

You directly provide us with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process it when you:

- Download and open the OpenBat app (UUID).
- Voluntarily, and optionally, provide a name to be added to your recordings.
- Voluntarily when you decide to upload recordings to our servers (location).

We may also receive your data indirectly from the following sources:

- When you submit an erasure request (UUID)

## How will we use your data?

Our Project collects your data so that we can:

- To credit you with, and track your recordings.
- To suggest AutoID models in your area.
- To contribute to Community Science Projects.

If you agree, we will share your data with anyone we partner with so that they may offer you their products and services.

- Your UUID (or name if given) and location at the time of recording will be shared with those making use of the recordings you submit. Recordings are only used for bat conservation purposes.

## How do we store your data?

We securely store your data via Cloudflare's cloud services. UUID's are stored in a D1 SQL database and recordings in R2 Object Storage.

We will keep your UUID on record until you ask us to remove it. This is so that when you do ask us to remove your data, we can accurately locate and delete all recordings you have submitted to us. We store the recordings (which contain your UUID, location at the time of recording, and name if given) you choose to upload for as long as the call is useful to our reference library.

You can request deletion of your data in app via the Privacy page in the settings menu.

## Marketing

We do not store contact information and do not engage in targeted marketing.

## What are your data protection rights?

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

**The right to access** – You have the right to request copies of your personal data. We may charge you a small fee for this service.

**The right to rectification** – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request that we complete information you believe is incomplete.

**The right to erasure** – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.

**The right to restrict processing** – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

**The right to object to processing** – You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

**The right to data portability** – You have the right to request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organisation, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at our email: privacy@openbat.app
