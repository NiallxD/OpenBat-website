Deer Cave runs through the limestone of Gunung Mulu in Malaysian Borneo, and the
passage is big enough that the scale only registers when something flies across
it.

The evening exodus is the draw. Where [[Bracken Cave]] empties in a single
turning column, the wrinkle-lipped bats here leave in long separated ribbons
that pulse out over the forest — a shape usually put down to the bat hawks
waiting outside, since a stream that keeps breaking and reforming is a harder
thing to pick from than a steady one.

The exodus doesn't happen every night. Rain cancels it, and a party that has
walked the boardwalk in the afternoon will sometimes sit at the observatory
until dark for nothing.
