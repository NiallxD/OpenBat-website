Most bats are nocturnal, which won't be a surprise to you I'm sure, but what do you know about the time when they actually head out for an evening of forraging?

## Roosting

During the day, bats typically roost in colonies of many individuals. These can be 10's of individuals up to millions! Different species will choose roost in different places, and will frequently roost with other species of bat too. 

Bat roosts range from the stereotypical caves to the furls in the bark of a cottonwood tree. Some species of bat love nothing more than a warm dry space in the roof of our homes, and some prefer to be in the narrow crevices in a mountain side. Common among all these roost places is that they are usually small, confined, and warm spaces. 

## Emergence

Bats which forage at night will typically leave their roost at sunset.
